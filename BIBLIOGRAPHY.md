# Bibliography: composition, colour and beauty

An annotated reading list behind `modules/composition/`, `modules/colour-value/` and the v0.5.0 rules in the other modules. Each entry has its source ID (full citation in `SOURCES.md`), where to read it, and one line on what it is good for.

**How to use it.** The rules paraphrase these works and link to them; short quotations come only from public-domain texts. Old books are wise but untested: the modules grade each claim (well supported, partly supported, craft wisdom, contested / myth; legend in `core/evidence.md`). Where an old book and an experiment disagree, the experiment wins for claims about viewers, and the book still wins as a source of methods to try.

**Rights notes.** Works published before 1931 are public domain in the United States. Elsewhere, the term is usually the author's life plus 70 years; check local law before reusing images or long passages. Links go to Project Gutenberg (gutenberg.org) or the Internet Archive (archive.org).

## 6. Empirical aesthetics: where to start

- **Overviews:** Palmer, Schloss & Sammartino 2013 [SRC-217]; Leder et al. 2004 and Leder & Nadal 2014 [SRC-258]; Wagemans et al. 2012 on Gestalt [SRC-250].
- **Golden ratio:** Green 1995 [SRC-220]; Höge 1997 [SRC-221]; Markowsky 1992 [SRC-222]; Falbo 2005 [SRC-223]; Fischler 1981 [SRC-224]; McManus et al. 2010 [SRC-38].
- **Balance:** McManus et al. 1985 [SRC-235]; Locher et al. 1996 and 1998 [SRC-236, SRC-263]; Wilson & Chatterjee 2005 and Hübner & Fillinger 2016 [SRC-237]; Gershoni & Hochstein 2011 [SRC-238].
- **Optical correction:** Material Design icon keylines [SRC-265].
- **Framing and direction:** Palmer et al. 2008 [SRC-218]; Sammartino & Palmer 2012 [SRC-219]; Chokron & De Agostini 2000 [SRC-254].
- **Attention and gaze:** Cerf et al. 2009 [SRC-239]; Friesen & Kingstone 1998 [SRC-240]; DeAngelus & Pelz 2009 [SRC-241]; Massaro et al. 2012 [SRC-243].
- **Complexity, order, novelty:** Nadal et al. 2010 [SRC-230]; Van Geert & Wagemans 2020 [SRC-231]; Hekkert et al. 2003 [SRC-232]; Reber et al. 2004 [SRC-37].
- **Curvature, symmetry, fractals:** Chuquichambi et al. 2022 [SRC-225]; Jacobsen & Höfel 2002 [SRC-234]; Spehar et al. 2003 and 2016 [SRC-228].
- **Colour:** Schloss & Palmer [SRC-41]; Ou & Luo 2006 [SRC-245]; Birch 2012 [SRC-252].
- **Whose taste:** Vessel et al. 2018 [SRC-251].
- **Stopping power (ads, eye-tracking):** Pieters & Wedel 2004 [SRC-333]; Pieters, Wedel & Batra 2010 [SRC-332], structured design versus pixel clutter. Energy pass (`core/energy-pass.md`).
- **Category cues and contrast on colour:** Piqueras-Fiszman & Spence 2015 [SRC-334], how packaging colour and pictures set taste expectations; Somers [SRC-335], black or white text on orange and red (practitioner). Energy pass items 3 and 8; CV-10.
- **Honest charts:** Pandey et al. 2015 [SRC-336], how truncated axes and distorted areas change what readers judge. Cleveland and McGill 1984 [SRC-337] and Spence and Lewandowsky 1991 [SRC-338] on choosing bars or pies. LAY-19, INF-4, `tools/datagraphic.py`.
