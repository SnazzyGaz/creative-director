# Cluster catalogue

Mixing several looks in one bleed instead of one. The test runs named here (cluster_01 to cluster_04) were small self-judged proofs made in the breeder; their images are not in this repo. Untested entries are predictions. Tags on wildcards are interpreted and unreviewed, as in the `interpreted` blocks in `looks.json`.

24 clusters: 8 alike (shared feel), 16 unlike (each look gives one layer). 10 tested in cluster_01/02, 14 untested predictions (self-judged). Lesson so far: unlike wins at 50%; alike is safer for a gentle 20%.
