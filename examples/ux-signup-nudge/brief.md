# Brief: library app sign-up form (test case)

Written for this stress test. The round-01 screens were built by hand in HTML to show common bot-made form mistakes. Not a real service.

- **Purpose:** let a library member create an account to reserve books.
- **Audience:** members of all ages, mostly on phones.
- **Main flow:** enter library card number, email and password, create the account.
- **Seen at:** 390 px phone.
- **Must-keeps (CD-5):** the three fields; the library's teal (#0f766e); the single-page form.
- **Done when:** someone who makes a typo can see what went wrong, fix it without retyping everything, and finish.
