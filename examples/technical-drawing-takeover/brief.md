# Brief: parcel box with a tin on top (test case)

Written for this stress test. Both drawings are **synthetic line drawings made by code** (`draw.py`), not hand drawings or AI images. Round 1 has errors planted on purpose so the measuring tools can be tested against known answers. `segments-rNN.csv` holds the true edge endpoints from the drawing code.

- **Purpose:** a product sketch showing a parcel box with a round tin standing on its lid, for a packaging mock-up.
- **Audience:** a packaging designer.
- **The one idea:** box and tin read as solid objects in believable two-point perspective, seen from above eye level.
- **Must-keeps (CD-5):** box corner toward the viewer; tin centred on the lid; line drawing, no shading.
- **Done when:** perspective, verticals and ellipses pass the TD rules when measured.
