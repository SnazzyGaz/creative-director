# Brief: "The keeper's cat" picture-book spread (test case)

Written for this stress test. Every image in this folder is AI-generated (GenerateImage). Round 1 used a deliberately bad "more is more" prompt to produce the generic AI look on purpose.

- **Purpose:** one spread in a picture book. The keeper is out at sea in a storm; the cat waits for the boat.
- **Audience:** children 4-7 and the adult reading aloud.
- **The one idea / focal point:** the cat, waiting and watching for the boat.
- **Seen at:** printed spread ~40 cm wide, and as a thumbnail in an online shop.
- **Must-keeps (CD-5):** cat, lighthouse, stormy sea at night, the keeper's small boat coming home.
- **Also needed:** a quiet area for two lines of text.
- **Done when:** at thumbnail size a reader finds the cat first and can tell it is waiting for the boat.
