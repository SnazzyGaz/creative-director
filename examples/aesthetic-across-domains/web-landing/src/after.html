What the six weeks cover

  1. Wedging and centring clay
  2. Throwing cylinders and bowls
  3. Trimming and handles
  4. Glazing your best pieces

Your pots are fired after the course and ready to collect two weeks later.

Who it is for

People who have never sat at a wheel, or tried once and want to learn properly. Wear clothes that can get muddy; aprons are provided.
