# Scoring form

One row per sheet in [`scoring.csv`](scoring.csv) (copy it, one copy per judge). The owner of the work fills in theirs first and alone.

| Column | What to write |
|---|---|
| `judge` | Your name, or the model name and version |
| `judge_type` | `owner`, `person` or `model` |
| `sheet` | Already filled: S01 to S16 |
| `left_id`, `right_id` | Already filled: the IDs printed under the two images |
| `preferred_id` | The ID of the piece you'd hand over for that brief, or `SAME` if you truly can't choose |
| `strength` | 1 slight, 2 clear, 3 strong |
| `reason` | A few words: what decided it |

**How to judge.** Look at the sheet as a whole, the way a client would: does it do what the brief asks, is the information right and readable, is it well made, would its audience respond to it. Go with your first reaction; don't zoom in hunting for flaws, and don't go back to change earlier answers. Each pair appears twice with the sides swapped; that's on purpose, just pick again.

**Paper version.** If you'd rather not use the CSV, write `S01 ABCD 2 calmer, easier to read` style lines in any text file; it can be typed into the CSV later.
