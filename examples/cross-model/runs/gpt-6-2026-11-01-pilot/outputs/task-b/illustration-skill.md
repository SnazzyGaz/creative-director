# Task B — skill-condition critique (reconstructed)

## What works
The lighthouse establishes place and the stormy sea establishes danger and distance. The cat's back-facing silhouette provides a plausible waiting pose. The warm lighthouse against the cold sea can support the emotional contrast.

## Blocker / highest priority
- **ILL-2 / CORE-3 — focal hierarchy:** the lamp and luminous sky dominate before the cat. At thumbnail size, the cat is a small dark shape against the foreground and does not clearly own the composition.
- **TD-LIT-1 — lighting coherence:** the lamp's radiating beams, lightning on multiple sides, glowing sky and many sparkle points compete as simultaneous light sources. Choose a primary light logic and subordinate effects.
- **ILL-6 — story readability:** the cat faces away and the boat is too small/ambiguous to immediately communicate “waiting for the keeper's boat.”
- **ILL-11 / CORE-8 / ILL-5 — restraint and hierarchy:** rainbow saturation, repeated sparkles, uniform detail and central symmetry produce a generic, over-effected finish.
- **ILL-4 / CORE-6 — colour roles:** too many high-saturation hues compete without a clear role system.

## Fixes, in order
1. Make the cat's silhouette, head direction and waiting posture more legible; strengthen the visual connection from cat to boat.
2. Reduce the sky's saturation and remove most sparkles/glow. Keep the storm, but choose one coherent main light source.
3. Increase the cat's contrast against the rocks, while keeping the lighthouse recognisable as a major supporting shape.
4. Simplify background edges/detail and preserve a quiet text area.
5. Check the composition at thumbnail size. The first read should be cat, second boat, third lighthouse/storm.

Do not “fix” hierarchy by deleting required story elements or making the cat implausibly huge. Judge the result against the story brief, not against a generic preference for muted art.

## Confidence
High confidence in the contrast/hierarchy and clutter observations. “Generic AI look” is subjective; the listed causes are more actionable than the label.
