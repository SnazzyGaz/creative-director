# Blind A/B judging sheet (run 01)

Twelve briefs. For each one there are two images, **A** and **B**, made from the same brief by two different processes. All 24 images were generated or coded by Grok Bot (an AI agent); none are by a human designer. You don't need to know anything about how they were made, and please don't look at other files in this repository until you have finished.

## How to judge (about 1 minute per pair)

1. Open the pair image, read the brief above it, then look at both versions at full size and at a small size.
2. Pick the one that is **stronger for this brief**: **A**, **B**, or **N** (neither is good enough).
3. Mark **strong** or **marginal**.
4. Optional: one line on why.

Judge the images only: the brief, what it is for and who it is for. There is no right answer and taste counts; if it's close, say marginal.

**Second pass (recommended, especially for AI judges):** a day later, or in a fresh session, judge [`pairs-swapped/`](pairs-swapped/). It shows the same pairs with the sides swapped (the letters stay with their images), to check whether the side something is on changed your choice.
