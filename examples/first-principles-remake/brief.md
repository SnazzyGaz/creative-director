# Step back and brief from first principles

The ask, for this example: "Rejig the seed-swap poster from scratch." That is a remake trigger (FP-1), so the light touch is not the default here. The starting point is the original in [`../aesthetic-across-domains/poster/`](../aesthetic-across-domains/poster/). Its light-touch fix is shown alongside for comparison.

## Fact check (FP-3)

- Date against weekday: consistent. 13 March 2027 is a Saturday (checked with Python's `datetime`; the calendar in concept C puts 13 in the S column).
- Time: 10:00 to 13:00. Consistent.
- Place: "in the hut", Hollin Lane Allotments (made up for these examples). Consistent.
- Anything added: no. The remake uses only the given words. "Bring spare seeds" and "take some home" are split across the two halves, word for word.

## Brief from first principles (FP-4): 7 lines

1. **What and where:** A3 type-only poster on a noticeboard and gate, read in passing at 2-4 m, then up close.
2. **For:** plot-holders and neighbours, many older, outdoors.
3. **One job:** get them to come on the day with spare seeds; the swap is the event.
4. **Feeling:** friendly, practical, sure of itself.
5. **Must survive:** every given fact word for word; type only; no invented copy; no logo needed.
6. **Free to change:** layout, type, colour, which line leads.
7. **Done when:** what, what to do and when read at 10% scale; every line passes contrast.


Sign-off: in a real hand-off this brief goes to the owner first and nothing is drawn until they agree (FLOW-1). In this example there is no owner, so it is marked **self-approved for the demonstration**.
