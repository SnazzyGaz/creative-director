# Brief: Crumb & Co bakery landing page (test case)

Written for this stress test. The round-01 page was deliberately built badly, by hand, to stand in for the worst kind of bot output. It is not a real business.

- **Purpose:** get local customers to pre-order weekend bread.
- **Audience:** neighbours, mostly on phones, often outdoors.
- **The one idea:** order by Thursday, collect on Saturday.
- **Seen at:** 390 px phone first, 1280 px desktop.
- **Must-keeps (CD-5):** the name "Crumb & Co"; the pre-order action; opening hours; the address; the brand orange (#E8743B); the three loaves on offer (sourdough, rye, seeded).
- **Done when:** a first-time visitor can tell what the place is and how to pre-order within a few seconds, and every text pair passes WCAG AA.
