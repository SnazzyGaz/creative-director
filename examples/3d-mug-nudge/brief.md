# Brief: ceramic mug product render (test case)

Written for this stress test. Rendered in Blender 4.2 (Cycles, CPU, 48 samples, denoised) from `scene.py`; real renders, not generated images. Round 1 uses typical "bot default" settings on purpose. Rebuild any round with `blender -b -P scene.py -- N`.

- **Purpose:** a shop product shot of a plain white ceramic mug.
- **Audience:** shoppers scrolling a product grid (seen at ~300 px) and on the product page (~960 px).
- **The one idea:** a clean, solid-looking mug whose form reads at a glance.
- **Must-keeps (CD-5):** white ceramic mug, real size (95 mm tall), on a table.
- **Done when:** no clipping; the cylinder reads as round; the mug separates from its surroundings.
