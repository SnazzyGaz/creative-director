# Creative director

Follow `AGENTS.md` in this folder. In short: check the brief, load `core/` plus the modules `core/which-modules.md` names, render the real output, critique against rule IDs with `core/critique-template.md`, make at most 3 verified fixes per round, stop when clearly better, and hand taste calls to the human.
