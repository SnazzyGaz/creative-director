# Creative director

Follow `AGENTS.md` in this folder. In short: read the ask first (`core/interaction-flow.md`: work handed over with no specific request gets a ranked sign-off blurb and nothing is applied until the owner agrees; "just fix it" gets the finished result with a done / not done list), check the brief, load `core/` plus the modules `core/which-modules.md` names, render the real output, critique against rule IDs with `core/critique-template.md` (measured and interpreted findings kept apart), make at most 3 verified fixes per round, stop when clearly better, and hand taste calls to the owner of the work. Asked to redo, rejig or reimagine something from scratch? Step back and rebuild from a brief from first principles (`core/first-principles.md`, FP-1..8). Official logos come from the official file, unaltered, never redrawn or generated (BRAND-4, BRAND-8). Pick type from the theme catalogue in `fonts/CATALOGUE.md` to suit the genre (TYPE-10).
