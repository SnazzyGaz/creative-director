# Decisions

## Decided by Gaz (2026-11-01)
1. **Single-subject framing:** no default. Centred and off-centre are equally valid; judge each case by intent (ILL-1, CORE-11, `core/evidence.md`). Myth notes on thirds and the golden ratio stay.
2. **ILL-8 mood conventions:** stay as a default, not a blocker. Only curved-vs-sharp has lab support [S42].
3. **UI-10 (text to 200% without breaking):** MUST-BE-RIGHT on every project.
4. **Gesture before structure:** moved from TD-CON-2 to illustration as **ILL-10**, a default, not a blocker. TD-CON-2 is left as a pointer.
5. **Taste-overrides list:** not created for now.

## Stress-test rule edits, approved by Gaz (2026-11-01)
1. **Critique loop, section 4:** check each fix applied; an unapplied fix counts as not done.
2. **Measure at zoom before calling a failure** (ellipses, verticals, alignment): `critique-loop.md` section 3, technical-drawing intro.
3. **Tools moved to top-level `tools/`** (`look.py`, `contrast.py`, `shot.sh`, `beforeafter.py`) with `tools/README.md`, referenced from the loop and the Auto notes. New: `vp.py` (least-squares VPs, horizon tilt, VP height mismatch, tilt of verticals) and `ellipse.py` (degree, minor axis vs axis line, end check).
4. **New ILL-11:** the generic AI image look (counterpart of UI-7).
5. **CORE-2:** very light sketch or line work is a deliberate exception when the brief calls for it.
6. **UI-7:** needs brief and brand input (CD-1, CD-2), not extra rounds.
7. **Section 4b:** take-over fires easily on AI images; a rework re-reads the brief (CD-5).
