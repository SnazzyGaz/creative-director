# Film: crossover map

Which rules from the rest of the repo carry into moving images. Three kinds: rules that apply **as they are** to any frame the viewer rests on; rules that **change in motion**; and rules that exist **only for film**. Judge "as they are" rules on key frames (the frame a shot starts, rests and ends on, and the frame on the beat), not on every frame in passing (FILM-COMP-1).

## Apply as they are (on key frames, or to every frame for blockers)

| Rule | In film |
|---|---|
| TD-PER-1 to TD-PER-3, TD-ELL, TD-PRO | Per frame. Horizons and verticals that bend over time are a generated-video fault (FILM-GEN-9). |
| TD-LIT-1 to TD-LIT-3, CV-7 | Per frame, and now across the cuts of a scene too (FILM-LIT-1). |
| CHAR-1 to CHAR-11 (anatomy pass CHAR-9) | On sampled frames, weighted to mid-gesture (FILM-GEN-3). |
| CV-1 to CV-3, CV-9 | Value plan, notan, not hue alone, colour-vision deficiency: on key frames. |
| CORE-2, CORE-3, ILL-2, ILL-5 | One focus per beat, read at blur and thumbnail, on key frames. |
| ILL-11 | Generic AI look; FILM-GEN-10 adds its motion defaults. |
| ILL-14, ILL-15, ILL-16 | Depth staging, framing within the picture, camera for the story: same ideas, now with blocking (FILM-COMP-3, FILM-STORY-2). |
| TYPE-3, LAY-15, UI-6 | Text size and contrast, checked on the worst frame behind the text over its duration (FILM-TXT-1). |
| LAY-4, LAY-5 | Export sizes and safe areas, plus broadcast safe areas (FILM-SHOT-5). |
| BRAND-4, BRAND-8 | Real marks only; invented text in generated video is a blocker (FILM-GEN-8). |
| AES-0 to AES-12, CD-1 | Intent, comparison and taste rules; FILM-STORY-0 is the moving-image form of AES-0. |
| 3D-1, 3D-2, 3D-7 | For rendered animation: built right, right scale, view transform that doesn't clip (FILM-LIT-5). |

## Change in motion

| Rule | What changes |
|---|---|
| COMP-1 to COMP-18 (placement, weight, eye path) | Motion and faces lead attention more than any static line [SRC-279, SRC-280]; placement is judged across the cut, not only within the frame (FILM-EDIT-2, FILM-COMP-2). |
| COMP-5 (room in front) | Becomes looking room and lead room, matched across shot/reverse-shot (FILM-SHOT-6, FILM-CON-3). |
| COMP-19 to COMP-25 (Sitting right) | Run on key frames only; balance resolves over time (a single is answered by its reverse). A flag, never a push to centre (FILM-COMP-4). |
| ILL-6 (one readable moment) | Becomes one readable beat per shot, and a chain of beats (FILM-STORY-0). |
| ILL-7 (intensity follows the story) | Becomes the intensity curve over time, with shot length and movement as extra channels (FILM-STORY-5). |
| CV-4 to CV-6 (colour in place, harmony, chroma budget) | Also planned across scenes as a colour script, and matched shot to shot (FILM-STORY-3, FILM-COL-1). |
| CORE-9 (consistency) | Becomes continuity: props, costume, light, look and identity across shots (FILM-CON-6, FILM-GEN-2). |
| CORE-10 (reads at real size) | Plus "reads in the time it is on screen" (FILM-EDIT-3, FILM-TXT-2). |
| The five look tests | Still useful on frames; add the in-motion review (FILM-REV-1). Stills alone are not a review. |
| UX-2 (visible status), UX-5 (a way out) | For interactive video and players: captions toggle, pause, scrub. |

## Film only

- Continuity conventions: the 180° line, the 30° rule, eyelines, screen direction, match on action (FILM-CON-1 to FILM-CON-6).
- Camera movement and its motivation; shutter and frame rate (FILM-CAM-1 to FILM-CAM-4).
- Lighting ratios and latitude across a scene (FILM-LIT-3, FILM-LIT-5).
- Grading pipeline and shot matching (FILM-COL-1 to FILM-COL-3).
- Cutting: priorities, eye-trace, shot length, J and L cuts, transitions, the Kuleshov effect (FILM-EDIT-1 to FILM-EDIT-6).
- Sound and music: sync, loudness, score, silence, bridges (FILM-SND-1, FILM-SND-2, FILM-STORY-7, FILM-STORY-8).
- Subtitles and flashing (FILM-TXT-2, FILM-ACC-1, FILM-ACC-2).
- Time-based storytelling: suspense and reveal, reaction and point of view, motifs (FILM-STORY-9 to FILM-STORY-11).
- Generated-video faults over time: identity drift, morphing, physics, flicker (FILM-GEN-2 to FILM-GEN-9).

## Myths that carry over

The same myths as for stills (`core/evidence.md`): thirds as a law, the golden ratio, fixed colour meanings, lines that force the eye. Film adds its own (`notes.md`, "Myths and weak claims").
