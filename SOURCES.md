# Sources

Every source cited in this repo. Ideas are paraphrased; no text or images are copied. Book references point to the publisher or a reputable description, not to scans. Numbers are grouped by topic, not in order. Cited as `[SRC-n]` (before v0.9.7, `[Sn]`; renamed so they never look like blind-test sheet IDs such as S88). They are never renumbered, and SRC-33 is retired.
